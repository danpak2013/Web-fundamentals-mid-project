Cookies Recipe

Ingredients: 1 cup butter, 1 cup sugar, 1 cup brown sugar, 2 eggs,2 tsp vanilla, 3 cups flour, 1 tsp baking soda, 1/2 tsp salt, 2 cups chocolate chips. Steps: Mix butter, sugars, eggs, and vanilla. Add flour, baking soda, and salt. Fold in chocolate chips. Scoop onto baking sheet. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-12 mins.

Cake Recipe

Ingredients: 2 sponge cake layers, 2 cups whipped cream, 1 cup strawberries (sliced), Whole strawberries for topping. Steps: Spread whipped cream on one sponge layer, add sliced strawberries. Place the second layer on top, cover with whipped cream. Decorate with whole strawberries.

Donuts Recipe

Ingredients: 2 cups flour, 1/2 cup sugar, 1 tsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 cup milk, 2 eggs, 1/4 cup butter (melted), Oil for frying, Sprinkles for topping. Steps: Mix flour,sugar,baking powder,and salt. Add milk, eggs, and butter. Knead into dough. Roll out and cut donut shapes. Fry in hot oil until golden. Cool and dip in icing. Decorate with sprinkles.
